# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, religion, or
other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Keep discussions focused on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
publishing private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation against a reporter are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub security-advisory form at
`https://github.com/IchoTolotos/carpool-matching-engine/security/advisories/new`, using a title that
begins with `Conduct report:`. Do not put sensitive conduct reports in public issues.

The maintainer will acknowledge a report, review available evidence, protect reporter privacy as
far as reasonably possible, and communicate an outcome. Responses may include a private warning,
content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Good-faith reports will not result
in retaliation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing
the project. It does not replace applicable law or platform policies.
